RDC Concrete (India) Limited stands as a pioneering and leading force in India's Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) industry. Established in 1993, we are proud to have set up India's first commercial RMC plant, marking the beginning of a revolution in construction practices across the nation. Today, we are the largest non-cement backed RMC manufacturer in India and the second largest RMC company overall by revenue (Fiscal Year 2025), a testament to our relentless pursuit of excellence and innovation.

With a vast and growing network of 140 RMC plants across the country (as of Jun 2025), RDC Concrete boasts an unparalleled pan-India presence. Each of our plants is equipped with fully automated and computerized batching systems, ensuring precise control over the production process, from raw material sourcing to final delivery. This meticulous approach guarantees that every batch of concrete meets the highest standards of quality and consistency, complying with all relevant Indian Standards.

With a fleet of over 67 own transit mixers, 32 own concrete pumps, 928 leased transit mixers and 148 leased concrete pumps as of May 2025, the company ensures seamless tracking integrated with our portal, 'RDCTRAK' to deliver efficiently  and living up to our Motto "We Promise, We Deliver". RDC Concrete's commitment to innovation and automation has established it as a trusted name in the Indian construction industry.

RDC is a Great Place to Work certified, consecutively for the fifth time, is among the Top 50 Mid-size Workplace to Work for in 2025 and Best place to Work in Cement and Building Material Industry.
