# RDC Concrete domain profile

## Scope

RDC Concrete (India) Limited operates in the Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) industry. The supplied company profile describes automated and computerized batching systems, raw-material-to-delivery operations, and integration with RDC operational tracking.

## Search terms

RDC Concrete, RDC, RMC, ready-mix concrete, ready mix, batching plant, automated batching, computerized batching, concrete delivery, transit mixer, concrete pump, RDCTRAK.

## How to use this document

Use this document to identify company and product context. It is background context, not a troubleshooting procedure. Do not use the company counts or rankings as current facts unless the source is re-verified; the supplied profile is dated June 2025 for plant information and May 2025 for fleet information.

## Supported operational boundary

Questions about concrete products, raw materials, mix designs, batching, plant operations, delivery, IDS Edge, Integrated Batching, Oracle ERP workflows, and their integration are within the support domain. Questions about unrelated general topics are outside the support domain.
